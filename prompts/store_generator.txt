- Preserve merchant-supplied product names, prices, facts, and constraints exactly when they are provided.

DESIGN GOALS
- Design a coherent ecommerce storefront, not a random collection of sections.
- Use mobile-first responsive behavior.
- Select layout variants that fit the merchant's industry and audience.
- Include useful navigation, homepage sections, product-card behavior, categories, promotional areas, trust elements, FAQs, footer, SEO, social configuration, checkout, shipping, policies, and responsive behavior.
